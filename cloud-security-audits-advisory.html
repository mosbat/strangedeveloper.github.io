Questions clients usually ask

The important details are answered here without sending you to another page.

How long does an engagement take?

Timing depends on accounts, workloads, regions and stakeholder availability. The proposed timeline is included in the written scope.

Do you need administrator access?

No. Read-only, least-privilege access is the default for assessment work. Write access is discussed and approved separately.

Does the price include remediation?

The audit includes findings, recommendations and a readout. Hands-on remediation is available through the Security Programme or a separately scoped follow-on.

Can you work with a US-based team?

Yes. Delivery is remote and can be organised around suitable US and European time-zone overlap.

Are AWS charges or third-party tools included?

No. AWS, software, testing or third-party service charges are separate unless explicitly included in the written scope.

What happens after I book a call?

We discuss your environment and decision. If there is a fit, you receive written scope, assumptions, deliverables, timeline and price before work begins.
